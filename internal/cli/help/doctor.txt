tkt doctor checks what the other commands need and reports each check as ok,
warn, fail or skip: git, worktrunk and herdr at their minimum versions, the
herdr pane, gh when the forge is GitHub, the harness of every preset, the
project's lifecycle configuration, config.toml, worktrunk's hooks and the log
directory. It exits 3 when a check fails.

On a terminal it groups the checks, colors them and ends with a summary.
Otherwise, and with --json, it prints the plain format that programs read.
