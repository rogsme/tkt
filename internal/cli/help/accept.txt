tkt accept takes the review at a ticket's head as it is, when its findings
do not matter, such as a remark on the pull request's wording. It comments
on the pull request:

  ## Review accepted

  Accepted head: `HEAD`
  Accepted review: CHANGES_REQUESTED on `HEAD`, submitted TIME
  Reason: REASON

finish-ticket and verify-pr take that comment in place of a clean review for
the head, and for a later head that only clean merges of the base follow, so
tkt sync keeps it. The human verification steps come from the pull request's
body, as after the review bot's CLEAR. tkt ls then shows the next step as
review_accepted, or verify-pr where agent verification is on, and tkt auto
and tkt merge go on as after a clean review. Any other commit, such as a
fix, voids the acceptance, and the next review runs as usual.

Only the newest review at the head can be accepted, and only one with
findings: FINDINGS from the review bot, or CHANGES_REQUESTED from
review-pr. tkt accept refuses while the ticket's agent works, since its push
would void the acceptance. --reason is required; the comment and
finish-ticket's completion comment record it. A second run on the same head
changes nothing.
