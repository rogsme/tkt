tkt stats reads the history tkt keeps in ${XDG_STATE_HOME:-~/.local/state}/tkt/history.jsonl
and shows, for this repository's tickets, how long each took from tkt start to
the merge, how much of that it waited for you (a plan to approve, a question,
the human verification), and how many address and ci rounds it needed.
Tickets still in progress show their time so far. The summary gives the
median and the 90th percentile of the merged ones.

tkt records the start and the merge exactly: the start when tkt start first
starts the ticket, the merge from the forge when tkt done removes it. Time in
each phase is as exact as the commands that watch it: tkt ls, tkt auto and the
tkt agent's board record a phase when they see it change.

The history outlives worktrees and only grows. Tickets started before tkt
kept a history have no recorded start and are left out.
