tkt hold puts a ticket on hold: tkt watch and tkt auto leave it alone until
tkt hold ID --off. Use it for a ticket you parked, or one you want to drive by
hand. The commands you run yourself, such as tkt run and tkt sync, still work
on it, and tkt ls still shows its next step.

A tkt auto loop that drives the ticket pauses while it is on hold, without a
notification, and goes on when the hold lifts. tkt ls shows hold in the AUTO
column and hold: true in --json.

The mark lives in the ticket's state, so it goes away with the worktree. tkt
history lists each time a ticket was put on hold and taken off it.
