tkt history shows what happened to each ticket you name, oldest first: every
event in the history tkt keeps in ${XDG_STATE_HOME:-~/.local/state}/tkt/history.jsonl
(the start, each phase, the plan's approval, each stage run, what tkt auto did
and why it stopped, the merge), and the timeline tkt stats counts from them.

On a GitHub forge, it also reads each pull request of the ticket's branch
through gh: its title, size, commits and reviews. A review's verdict and the
head it names come from review-pr's Verdict: line or the review bot's
summary, and the body holds its findings. Reviews with only line comments
and no body are left out. When gh fails, tkt warns, prints the history
alone, and forge says failed.

A ticket keeps its history after tkt done removes its worktree, so this
works for merged tickets too. tkt history is read only. The tkt agent reads it
to answer how a ticket went.
