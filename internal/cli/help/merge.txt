tkt merge hands a ticket whose human verification passed to finish-ticket.
It sends a fresh session in the ticket's tab the line

  Use the finish-ticket skill to merge the PR. Human verification steps 1
  to 3 passed.

finish-ticket then checks CI, the review and the feedback at the exact
head, merges, waits for post-merge CI, completes the ticket in the tracker,
and ends with Status: DONE. tkt watch removes the ticket after that.

--passed names the steps that passed: numbers and ranges such as 1-3 or
1,2,4, or all. finish-ticket merges only when every step passed, so tkt
merge refuses a list that leaves one out; to waive a step, tell the agent
in the ticket's tab yourself. --head names the head the steps ran on, and
tkt merge refuses once the pull request moved past it. It also refuses a
head no review cleared, a branch behind its base (finish-ticket would stop
with BLOCKED_FOR_SYNC; sync, then verify the new head), and failing
checks.

While the checks at the head still run, tkt merge leaves a merge order
instead, and tkt watch sends the line once they pass. tkt ls shows the
order in the merge field, and the tkt agent's board as merges when green. A
new head drops it, with a notification, since the steps ran on the old one;
a base that moves meanwhile gets a sync first, which makes a new head too.
--cancel drops the order.
