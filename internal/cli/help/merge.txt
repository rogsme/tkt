tkt merge hands a ticket whose human verification passed to finish-ticket.
It sends a fresh session in the ticket's tab the line

  Use the finish-ticket skill to merge the PR. Human verification steps 1
  to 3 passed on head HEAD.

finish-ticket then checks CI, the review and the feedback at the exact
head, merges, waits for post-merge CI, completes the ticket in the tracker,
and ends with Status: DONE. tkt watch removes the ticket after that.

--passed names the steps that passed: numbers and ranges such as 1-3 or
1,2,4, or all. finish-ticket merges only when every step passed, so tkt
merge refuses a list that leaves one out; to waive a step, tell the agent
in the ticket's tab yourself. --head names the head the steps ran on. The
steps hold across clean merges of the base, the ones tkt sync makes, so tkt
merge refuses only a head that changed beyond them. Before any session
starts, it also refuses a head no review cleared, a base whose merge
conflicts (the steps never carry over a resolution: sync, then verify the
new head) and failing checks. finish-ticket checks the rest itself, such as
a review that says READY_FOR_HUMAN at the head: when only the review bot
cleared it, tkt merge warns that finish-ticket stops with Status: BLOCKED
without one.

When finish-ticket could not merge yet, tkt merge leaves a merge order
instead, on the --as preset if you named one, and tkt watch carries it out.
A branch behind its base gets a clean sync first, then a review of the new
head; the order waits for running checks, and for another ticket's merge,
since one goes at a time. The line then names the head the steps passed on
and the one to merge, and finish-ticket checks the merges between them
itself. tkt ls shows the order in the merge field, and the tkt agent's
board as merges when green.

The order stands until the pull request merges. When finish-ticket stops
with BLOCKED_FOR_SYNC because another merge moved the base, tkt watch syncs
again and sends the line on the new head; on one head it sends the line
once. A new head of any other kind, such as a fix or the resolution of a
conflict, drops the order, with a notification, since the steps ran on code
it changed.

--cancel drops the order. tkt merge holds the ticket's lock until the line
is sent, so --cancel either drops the order before or finds the line sent:
it then says when, and stopping finish-ticket is up to you, in the
ticket's tab.
